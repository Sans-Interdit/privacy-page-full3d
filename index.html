Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our application ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

The App may request access to the following:

  • Camera: Used to capture photos of broken objects.
  • Photos / Media: Used to upload images for 3D modeling requests.

We collect personal data such as email address, authentication identifiers (Google login or email/password), and images submitted for 3D printing requests.

Some user data is stored securely on our servers to process quotes, orders, and delivery of 3D printed items.

2. How We Use Information

Any data accessed through your device (such as camera usage) is used solely to provide core features of the App.

We do not:

  • Sell your data
  • Share your data with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising purposes

3. Permissions

The App may request the following permission:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required to use camera-related features.

You can deny this permission, but some features may not function properly.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for authentication, data storage, email delivery, and order processing.

These services are only used to operate the core functionality of the App.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

7. Account and Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

To request account deletion, users can:

  • Send an email to: yanis@somemark.fr

Once a deletion request is received, the following data will be removed:

  • User account information (email, Google account ID)
  • Order and quote history
  • Uploaded images and 3D request data

Some data may be retained for legal or accounting obligations for up to 90 days.

Data deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:

yanis@somemark.fr
